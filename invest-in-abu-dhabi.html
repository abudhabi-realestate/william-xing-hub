六个投资阿布扎比的理由

结构性优势，而非一时行情

TAX

零个税 · 零资本利得税

无个人所得税，出售房产无资本利得税，无年度房产税；仅在过户时缴纳约 2% 的一次性 ADREC 过户费（迪拜为 4%）。

GV

黄金签证门槛清晰

房产投资达 AED 200 万即可申请 10 年期黄金签证，直系家属可随同办理。

查看黄金签证常见问题 →
FX

迪拉姆锚定美元

AED 与美元挂钩超过 30 年，固定汇率 3.6725，资产端不承受额外汇率波动风险。

OWN

外国人可 100% 自由持有

在 Reem / Yas / Saadiyat / Al Maryah 等指定投资区，外国人可 100% 永久产权，无需本地担保人，产权直接登记。

ROI

有竞争力的租金回报

主流板块毛租金回报率约 5%–8%（Reem / Yas 约 6%–8%，Saadiyat 约 5%–7%），高于伦敦（3%–4%）、纽约（4%–5%）、巴黎（3%–4%）等成熟市场。

2030

城市升级持续兑现

三岛基建、文化、金融与体育设施投入不断落地，早期入场窗口仍在打开。

Six Reasons to Invest in Abu Dhabi

Structural advantages, not a one-quarter rally

TAX

Zero income & capital gains tax

No personal income tax, no capital gains tax on resale, no annual property tax — only a one-time ~2% ADREC transfer fee at closing (vs 4% in Dubai).

GV

Clear Golden Visa threshold

A property investment of AED 2M qualifies you for a renewable 10-year Golden Visa, with immediate family included.

Read the Golden Visa FAQ →
FX

AED pegged to the US dollar

Fixed at 3.6725 for over three decades — no extra currency risk sitting on top of your property exposure.

OWN

100% freehold for foreigners

In designated investment zones — Reem, Yas, Saadiyat, Al Maryah and others — foreign nationals hold outright title, no local sponsor required.

ROI

Competitive rental yields

Gross yields of roughly 5%–8% in mainstream communities (Reem/Yas ~6%–8%, Saadiyat ~5%–7%) — ahead of London (3%–4%), New York (4%–5%) and Paris (3%–4%).

2030

Vision 2030 keeps delivering

Infrastructure, culture, finance and sports investment across the three islands continues to land — the early-mover window is still open.
